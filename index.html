Public Firebase synchronization

Connecting to the shared Firebase project…

No login or user roles

Everyone with this app link can view and submit weighings from their phone or computer. New entries are kept offline on each device and uploaded when internet access returns.

Public data warning

Public Firestore rules mean the records can also be accessed or submitted by people who discover your Firebase project, even without the app link. Use only if drivers’ names, weighing information, and the public write risk are acceptable.

Local storage & backups

Back up before clearing your browser data. Import merges by record ID and preserves original entries; conflicting fleet masters are not automatically overwritten.

Do not clear browser storage or uninstall the app while records are unsynced and not backed up.
